The Willow Learning enquiry concept

This page explains, in plain terms, how tutoring enquiries work today, what the evidence review found, and how this improved concept helps. It is based on a small review of anonymized tutoring enquiry journeys, assessed on three dimensions: findability, request burden/transparency, and next-step clarity.

The current journey

Today, enquiries for Willow Learning are handled ad hoc by email. The previous site had no dedicated enquiry call-to-action, service options were not published, and there was no confirmation message or expected response time. Across the comparator journeys we reviewed, the same pattern repeated: enquiry links buried in footers or deep sub-pages, long forms demanding details (like full postal addresses) that aren't needed for a first reply, and silence after submission — no confirmation, no timeline.

What works

The best comparator experiences shared three traits: a visible enquiry button in the top navigation or hero, a short form that shows indicative pricing up front, and a confirmation that states exactly when and how you'll hear back. Short, honest intake forms get completed; transparency builds trust before the first conversation.

What needs improvement

Willow Learning's ad-hoc email process fails on all three dimensions: hard to find, undefined in what to send, and unclear about what happens next. The main improvement opportunity is a single, prominent, low-burden enquiry path with a guaranteed, stated next step.

How the improved concept helps

The concept pairs this website with a short prototype intake form. It asks only the minimum useful information — learner level, subject, goals, availability, and your preferred reply method (with conditional email or phone capture). Every submission ends with an explicit confirmation. For prospective families and learners, that means: one obvious place to start, five honest questions, and certainty about what happens next.
